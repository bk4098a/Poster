Introduction

  • This poster explores how race and socio-economic background predict future income outcomes in the U.S.
  • Using Opportunity Insights data, we evaluate correlations between race, parental income, and adult income rank.
  • Statistical and machine learning models are used to evaluate predictive performance.

Methods

  • Data: U.S. Census Public Use Data from Opportunity Insights
  • Sample size: 64 observations
  • Key variables: par_pctile, count_black_pooled, kir_1par_black_male, kir_1par_white_male

\[ IncomeRank = f(ParentIncome, BlackPopulation) \]
